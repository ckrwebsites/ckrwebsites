Hi, I'm Charrie.

I build websites for small businesses — and then I stick around and look after them.

CKR Websites started the way a lot of good small businesses do: helping family. I built a website for my sister's painting company in Florida, then took over my aunt's dog breeding site in Texas, then built one for my own breeding program here in Windsor. Word travels, and friends and family kept asking — so here we are.

I'm not an agency. When you hire CKR Websites, you get me: the person who designs your site is the person who answers your email at 9pm because your hours changed and the website needs to say so. I care about the unglamorous parts other builders skip — whether Google can find you, whether your photos load fast, whether the words on the page answer the questions your customers are actually asking.

I'm based in Windsor, Ontario, and my clients are in Ontario, Florida, Texas and beyond. If your business needs a website — or your current website needs a person — I'd love to hear about it.

Ready for a website that works as hard as you do?
