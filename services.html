Questions

How long does a website take?

Most Launch and Grow sites are live within two to three weeks once I have your words and photos. Bigger projects get a timeline up front.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content, your site. If you ever move on, everything goes with you.

Can you fix or take over my existing website?

Usually, yes. Send me the address and I'll take a look and tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or better rebuilt.

Where are you located?

I'm in Windsor, Ontario, and I work with clients across Canada and the United States — most of my clients I've never met in person, and their sites run just fine.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo if you have one, the words you want to say (I can help), and photos of your work. Then I take it from there.

Ready for a website that works as hard as you do?

Tell me about your business and I'll tell you honestly what it needs — and what it doesn't.
